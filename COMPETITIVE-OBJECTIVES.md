# GoreeCloud Memos — Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Memos should differentiate through a deliberately narrow product focus rather than feature count.

Objectives are to make capture fast, keep self-hosting and privacy first-class, preserve user-owned portable data, provide reliable offline/cross-device behavior, recover cleanly from failures, and integrate with the wider GoreeCloud ecosystem without requiring external accounts or hosted dependencies.

The current development slice is not yet suitable for comparative performance or feature claims. Benchmarks and competitive assertions require measured evidence before publication.
