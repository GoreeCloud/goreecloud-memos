# GoreeCloud Memos — Benefits

This file describes the value of GoreeCloud Memos as the lightweight quick-capture application. Benefits tied to post-v0.1.2 source work or client candidates remain acceptance-gated.

## Fast capture

- Provides a deliberately lightweight place for short notes, snippets, reminders, lists, and ideas without requiring the deeper organization of a full knowledge-management application.
- Keeps common actions such as creating, editing, pinning, labeling, searching, archiving, restoring, and deleting notes close to the primary workflow.
- Markdown-friendly content and checklists support useful structure without forcing a heavy editor or notebook model.

## Simple organization and retrieval

- Labels/tags, search, pinning, archive, per-note colors, and recoverable Trash make information easier to find and manage while preserving a compact mental model.
- Attachments and inline images are available when useful without redefining Memos as a document-management system.

## Privacy and ownership

- Runs as a private GoreeCloud-hosted service instead of requiring a proprietary consumer-cloud note account.
- Uses individual accounts and private-by-default visibility.
- Avoids making advertising, sponsorship, analytics, or an external AI provider part of the core note experience.
- Keeps the authoritative memo database and service under GoreeCloud control even when Linux or Android clients are used.

## Recovery and reliability

- Recoverable Trash, archive/restore behavior, recurring backup coverage, monitoring, restart-persistence validation, immutable release artifacts, and rollback support reduce the risk that a quick note becomes disposable simply because the interface is lightweight.
- Production and source/release states are tracked separately so unaccepted changes are not silently treated as deployed behavior.
- The accepted production deployment preserves TLS, private DNS, controlled Caddy routing, non-root runtime hardening, data persistence, and recovery history.

## Cross-device access

- Responsive web/PWA behavior provides a common lightweight interface across screen sizes.
- The first-party Tauri client direction allows Linux and Android access while continuing to use the same authoritative GoreeCloud Memos service instead of introducing conflicting local databases.
- One canonical product icon and GoreeCloud identity make the application recognizable across supported client surfaces.

## Product clarity

- Memos remains intentionally separate from GoreeCloud Notes. Users can choose fast capture in Memos or deeper long-term knowledge work in Notes without forcing either application to become unnecessarily complex.
- Future interoperability can promote a memo into Notes when a quick thought becomes durable knowledge while preserving clear data ownership and user control.

## Long-term benefit

GoreeCloud Memos aims to provide the speed and simplicity associated with products such as Google Keep and Apple Notes while adding self-hosting, privacy, data ownership, recoverability, transparent release engineering, and first-party GoreeCloud control.
