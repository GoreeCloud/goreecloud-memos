# GoreeCloud Memos — Benefits

## Verified current benefits

The current development slice provides a small local quick-capture workflow with no required account or network service. Saved memo content stays in the browser's IndexedDB and draft text stays in local browser storage.

## Planned product benefits

The governing roadmap targets fast capture, privacy by default, self-hosting, cross-device access, offline-capable official clients, open/portable data, tested recovery, and interoperability with GoreeCloud Notes without duplicating Notes' deeper knowledge-management role.

These planned benefits are objectives, not claims about the current repository state.
